Description

Stack status effects. Detonate them. A pixel-art action game where you pile Burn, Freeze, Shock, Bleed and Mark onto your enemies, then swap weapons mid-combo to blow them all up at once. Build around 60 weapons, fight 15 bosses across one connected underground world — and when you fall, your weapons stay where you fell.

Features

  • Stack, then detonateEvery weapon inflicts something different. Make them bleed with a thorn whip, set them alight with an ember blade, freeze them with an icicle dagger. Then land a finisher with a weapon that can detonate — and watch it all go off. Fire spreads. Ice shatters. Lightning arcs.
  • Three weapons, one comboCarry three weapons and swap at any time without breaking your combo. Pair the weapon that applies a status with the one that detonates it.
  • Build your way60 weapons across six types (swords, quick blades, heavy weapons, polearms, scythes, staves), 12 armors and 39 accessories. Some status effects combine in ways the game won't tell you about.
  • One connected worldFrom the prison where you wake to the light of the surface, 13 regions form a single map. Secret rooms, trap rooms, and sentinels guarding the bosses' halls.
  • Fall, and you drop itWhen you die, every weapon but your starting sword stays where you fell. Wake at your base and go back for it.
  • 15 bossesRead their tells, break their guard, tear them down with detonations.
  • And beyondNew Game+ loops with tougher enemies and rarer weapons, 26 memory fragments and a true ending, boss rematches with records, and achievements. Rescue the people of the underground to grow your base.

How AI was used

ECHOBOUND is developed with extensive use of AI (Claude Code). It wrote the code, the in-game text and its translations, and the programs that draw the pixel art and synthesize the sound.

The art, sound effects and music are not output from image or music generation models. They are produced by those programs, following rules. The game's design, its specifications, and every decision about what goes in were made by the developer.
